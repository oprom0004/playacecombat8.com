Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve Editions & Pricing

Ace Combat 8 is available on Steam (PC), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S in three editions: Standard Edition ($69.99), Deluxe Edition ($89.99 with Season Pass + 6 Bonus DLC Aircraft), and Wings of Theve Collector's Edition ($149.99 with die-cast F-22A model).
