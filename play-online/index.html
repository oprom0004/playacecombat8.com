Combat Flight Tactics & Flight Controls

Master the art of energy fighting and high-G turns in Ace Combat 8:

1. Lead Pursuit & Cannon Aim

Lead your crosshairs slightly ahead of the enemy jet's vector to ensure your 20mm Vulcan shells connect.

2. Heat-Seeking Missile Lock

Keep hostile bogeys inside the central crosshair circle until the HUD flashes MISSILE LOCK before firing.

3. Afterburner Energy Fighting

Engage afterburners (Shift / AB) to close distance rapidly, or cut throttle into tight High-G scissor maneuvers.
