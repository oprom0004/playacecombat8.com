How to Fix Ace Combat 8 PC & Steam Deck Crashing

FIX 01

Bypass DirectX 12 Crash

Unreal Engine 5 shader compilation occasionally panics on older GPU drivers and Proton. Right-click Ace Combat 8 in Steam -> Properties -> General -> In Launch Options, paste:

-dx11 -useallavailablecores
FIX 02

Update Proton to GE-Proton 9

If the game freezes during Mission 04 cutscenes: Open Steam Deck Settings -> Compatibility -> Force the use of Proton Experimental or install GE-Proton 9-10 via ProtonUp-Qt.

FIX 03

Clear Corrupt Shader Cache

Navigate to ~/.steam/steam/steamapps/shadercache/2288340/ on Linux or delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\BandaiNamcoAces\AceCombat8\Saved\ cache folder on Windows.
